Questions About Our Talent

Everything you need to know about booking our professional entertainers.

Can I request a specific entertainer by name? +
Absolutely! While all our entertainers are incredible, if you fell in love with Elias or Princess Lily based on their profiles or a previous party, you can request them specifically in the booking form. Availability is first-come, first-served.
What happens if my requested entertainer gets sick? +
We always have backup entertainers on standby for every single weekend. If your specific entertainer is unable to perform due to an emergency, we will instantly dispatch an equally qualified performer so your party never misses a beat.
Do they bring their own props and equipment? +
Yes! Our entertainers arrive fully equipped. Magicians bring their illusions and sound systems, Princesses bring their storybooks and music, and Clowns bring their balloon kits. You don't need to provide anything but the space!
Do I need to tip the entertainer? +
Tipping is completely optional but always highly appreciated by our performers. If you feel they went above and beyond to make your child's day magical, a standard tip is usually between 15% and 20%.
