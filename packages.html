Frequently Asked Questions

Are the displayed package prices final?

The prices shown are sample starting prices for our standard packages. The final cost may vary slightly depending on your exact location, the specific entertainer you choose, and any optional add-ons you select during the booking process.

How early should I book a package?

We highly recommend booking at least 3-4 weeks in advance, especially for weekend events or holidays, as our most popular entertainers' schedules fill up quickly. Booking early gives you maximum flexibility for your preferred date and time.

Can packages be customized for unique events?

Absolutely! All our packages serve as a magical foundation. When you proceed to the booking page, you will find a "Customize Your Experience" section where you can add special requests, extra entertainers, or unique themes.
