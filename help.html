Help Center

Need assistance? Find answers to common questions and learn how to get the most out of Magiqo.

1. How to Book an Entertainer

Browse our categories, select an entertainer, choose a package, and click 'Book Now'. You'll be prompted to enter your event details and secure the booking with a payment.

2. Managing Your Bookings

You can view, modify, or cancel your upcoming events by logging into your account and navigating to the 'My Bookings' tab in your user dashboard.

3. Payment Issues

If your payment is declined, please ensure your card details are correct or try an alternative payment method. For billing inquiries, contact our support team.

4. Contact Support

If you still need help, our dedicated support team is available 24/7. Please visit our Contact page to send us a message, or email us directly at support@magiqo.com.
