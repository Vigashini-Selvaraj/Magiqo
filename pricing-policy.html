Pricing Policy

Learn about our transparent pricing, booking fees, and refund policies for all magical entertainment.

1. Transparent Pricing

All entertainer packages on Magiqo are priced upfront. What you see is what you pay. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges after your booking is confirmed.

2. Booking Fees & Deposits

A small service fee is applied to each booking to maintain the Magiqo platform and provide 24/7 customer support. Some entertainers may require a non-refundable deposit to secure your date, which will be clearly stated during checkout.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

If you cancel a booking at least 7 days before the event, you will receive a full refund (minus any non-refundable deposit). Cancellations made within 7 days of the event may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

4. Overtime Charges

If your event runs longer than the booked time, overtime charges may apply at the entertainer's standard hourly rate, billed in 30-minute increments.
