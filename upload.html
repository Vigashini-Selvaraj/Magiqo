Upload Guidelines

Help us keep our community space family-friendly, safe, and inspiring:

  • Accepted Media: High-res JPG, PNG, and MP4 videos (up to 25MB each).
  • Safe Moderation: All uploads are reviewed by our team within 24 hours prior to public gallery display.
  • Parental Consent: Ensure you have full permission from parents of all children clearly featured in footage.
  • Privacy Guarantee: We never sell personal photos or license them to external third parties.
