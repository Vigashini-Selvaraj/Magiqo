Answers to Your Questions

How far in advance should I contact you?

We recommend reaching out at least 4-6 weeks before your desired event date to ensure the best entertainer availability.

Can I customize a standard package?

Absolutely! Just fill out the form above with your custom requests and our planning team will send a tailored quote.

Are there cancellation fees?

You can cancel for free up to 7 days before the event. Any cancellations within 7 days are subject to a non-refundable deposit.
