Booking FAQs

When is my advance payment due?
A 20% advance payment is required at the time of booking to secure your date. The remaining balance is due 24 hours before the event.
Can I modify my booking later?
Yes! You can add extra guests, add-ons, or even change your entertainer up to 7 days before your event, subject to availability.
What is the cancellation policy?
You can cancel for a full refund up to 14 days before the event. Cancellations within 14 days will forfeit the 20% deposit.
