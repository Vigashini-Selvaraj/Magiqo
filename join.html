Career FAQs

Do you hire freelancers for events?
Yes! The majority of our entertainers, photographers, and caterers operate on a gig/freelance basis. You can pick up events that match your schedule.
Are there internship opportunities?
Absolutely. We offer paid summer internships for event management and marketing students looking to get hands-on experience in the entertainment industry.
What is the audition process like for entertainers?
If your resume/portfolio is shortlisted, we'll invite you for a 15-minute live or virtual audition where you can perform your best routine for our panel.
Where is Magiqo located?
Our core planning team works hybrid in our downtown office, but our entertainers operate throughout the entire tri-state area.
